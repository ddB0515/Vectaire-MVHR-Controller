Home Assistant

Connect the controller to your MQTT broker. Home Assistant finds it by itself (MQTT discovery). · Back

On the controller's screen under Menu › Device: tap the Firmware row. Without a screen: on the setup page (press the button for 1–4 s).

Unlocking here or on the home page unlocks both, for this browser tab only.

How often temperatures, humidity and fan levels are sent. Changes such as fan speed, modes, boost or alarms are sent at once.

Leave the broker address empty to switch Home Assistant off. The controller restarts after saving.
